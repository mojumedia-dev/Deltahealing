What is Ketamine Therapy?

Ketamine is a medication that has been used in medicine for decades as an anesthetic and, more recently, in supervised low-dose settings as a treatment option for adults with depression that has not responded to standard antidepressants. It works differently than SSRIs and SNRIs by acting on the NMDA glutamate system, which may help support the brain in forming new patterns connected to mood, stress, and emotional processing.

At Delta Healing Solutions, ketamine therapy is administered in-clinic under monitoring by our medical team. It is a cash-pay service and is not billed to insurance.

Who Ketamine Therapy May Help

Ketamine therapy may be considered for adults who:

  • Have been diagnosed with major depressive disorder or treatment-resistant depression
  • Have tried two or more antidepressant medications without adequate improvement
  • Cannot tolerate the side effects of standard antidepressants
  • Are looking for a treatment option with a different mechanism of action
  • Have completed a psychiatric evaluation and been screened for appropriateness
Not a first-line treatment. Ketamine therapy at Delta Healing Solutions is reserved for adults who have already tried standard treatments. All patients complete a psychiatric evaluation before starting.

What to Expect at Your Ketamine Session

Before Your Session

  • Consultation and psychiatric evaluation with our provider
  • Screening for medical and psychiatric contraindications
  • Arrange for someone to drive you home after each session
  • Follow any pre-session eating and medication instructions we give you

During Your Session

  • You will be in a comfortable, private clinical space
  • Our clinical team monitors blood pressure and response throughout
  • Many patients report feeling relaxed, sleepy, lightheaded, emotionally sensitive, or somewhat disconnected from surroundings during the session
  • Sessions last approximately 60-90 minutes including monitoring

After Your Session

  • Monitoring continues until you are safe to leave
  • A designated driver must take you home; you cannot drive after a session
  • Most acute effects resolve within a few hours
  • Plan a low-demand rest of the day

Safety, Side Effects, and Who Should Not Receive Ketamine

Common temporary side effects during or shortly after a session may include:

  • Feeling relaxed, sleepy, or lightheaded
  • Feeling emotionally sensitive
  • Feeling disconnected from usual thoughts or surroundings (dissociation)
  • Elevated blood pressure or heart rate
  • Nausea
Ketamine therapy is not appropriate for everyone. Contraindications include uncontrolled hypertension, unstable cardiovascular disease, active substance use disorder involving ketamine or dissociatives, active psychotic disorder, pregnancy, and certain other medical or psychiatric conditions. Our team screens for these during your evaluation.
