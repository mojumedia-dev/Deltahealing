Why Your Gift Matters

Delta Healing Solutions is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. We built our clinic around a combination-treatment model: TMS, ketamine-assisted therapy, medication management, psychiatric evaluation, and coordination with community talk therapy. That model works, and our PHQ-9 outcomes show it: 82.1% response, 61.3% remission, 93.7% completion across 126 TMS patients.

The one gap is affordability for patients without commercial insurance. That is where donations go.

Your donation is 100% tax-deductible and directly supports non-profit cash pricing for patients without commercial coverage, keeps the combination-treatment model available, and expands community mental health access in Millard County.
