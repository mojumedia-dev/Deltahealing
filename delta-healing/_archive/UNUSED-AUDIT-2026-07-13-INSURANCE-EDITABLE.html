Insurance FAQ

How do I find out if my plan covers TMS?

Call us at (435) 864-5151 with your insurance card. Our specialist verifies your benefits, usually the same day, and tells you what to expect for out-of-pocket costs.

Do I need a referral?

Most plans do not require a referral for psychiatric evaluation or medication management. TMS typically requires prior authorization, which we handle. Some HMO-style plans require a PCP referral, and we can tell you which.

What if my plan is not on the list?

Call anyway. Plan networks change, and we may still be in-network or able to offer out-of-network options. If neither works, we can quote non-profit cash pricing.

You said Medicaid is not accepted. What are my options?

Our non-profit cash pricing was built specifically for patients without commercial coverage. Call us for a plain estimate and to discuss payment plans that fit your situation.

Is ketamine-assisted therapy covered by insurance?

Ketamine is offered on a cash-pay basis and is not billed to insurance. We can provide detailed pricing and, in many cases, combine ketamine with insurance-covered TMS and medication management as one plan.

How long does prior authorization take?

Most TMS prior authorizations come back within 5-10 business days. Complex cases can take longer. We keep you updated at every step.
