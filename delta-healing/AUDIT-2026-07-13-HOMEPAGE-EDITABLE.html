What is TMS therapy?

TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation) is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment for depression using magnetic pulses. Delta Healing Solutions offers NeuroStar TMS for patients who have not responded to traditional antidepressants. TMS is often planned alongside talk therapy for stronger, more durable results.

What is ketamine-assisted therapy?

Ketamine-assisted therapy is a medically supervised treatment for patients who have not found enough response through traditional antidepressants. At Delta Healing Solutions, ketamine is administered in-clinic under monitoring and is most effective when combined with TMS and talk therapy. It is cash-pay only, not billed to insurance.

Is treatment at Delta Healing Solutions confidential?

Yes. Every visit is confidential and stays between you and our team. We understand the concerns of a small community and take privacy seriously. Discretion is part of what we do.
