NeuroStar TMS Therapy

What is NeuroStar TMS therapy?

NeuroStar TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation) is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive treatment for depression. It uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate underactive brain regions associated with mood regulation, particularly the left dorsolateral prefrontal cortex. TMS does not require anesthesia, and patients can drive themselves to and from appointments.

Who is a good candidate for TMS?

TMS is typically appropriate for adults with Major Depressive Disorder or treatment-resistant depression who have not achieved adequate relief from antidepressant medications, or who cannot tolerate medication side effects. Delta Healing Solutions completes a formal medical appropriateness and insurance eligibility determination for every referral.

How long is a course of TMS therapy?

A standard course is 36 sessions delivered over roughly 6 to 9 weeks, typically 5 days per week. Individual sessions last approximately 19 to 37 minutes depending on the protocol.

What are the outcomes of TMS therapy at Delta Healing Solutions?

Between March 2024 and December 2025, Delta Healing Solutions treated 126 patients with TMS therapy. Of those, 118 patients (93.7%) completed an adequate course of 30 or more sessions. 82.1% achieved clinical response, defined as at least a 50% reduction in baseline PHQ-9 score, and 61.3% achieved remission, defined as a final PHQ-9 score of 4 or lower.

Is TMS safe?

TMS has a strong safety profile. The most common side effect is mild scalp discomfort or headache during treatment, which typically resolves after the first week. Contraindications include ferromagnetic implants near the head, active mania, and history of seizure disorder. Every patient is screened for these before treatment.

Ketamine Therapy

What is ketamine therapy at Delta Healing Solutions?

Ketamine therapy is a medically supervised in-clinic treatment option for adults with depression that has not responded to standard antidepressants. Ketamine acts through the NMDA glutamate system rather than the serotonin system targeted by SSRIs and SNRIs. All ketamine therapy at Delta Healing Solutions is administered under monitoring by the clinical team.

Is ketamine therapy covered by insurance at Delta Healing Solutions?

No. Ketamine therapy at Delta Healing Solutions is offered on a cash-pay basis only and is not billed to insurance. Call (435) 864-5151 for current pricing.

What is the difference between ketamine therapy and NeuroStar TMS?

NeuroStar TMS is a non-invasive, non-medication treatment that uses magnetic pulses and is typically covered by insurance. Ketamine therapy is a medication-based treatment delivered under in-clinic supervision and is cash-pay only. Both are options for adults with depression that has not responded to standard antidepressants. The clinical team helps patients decide which is a better fit during the psychiatric evaluation.

Can I drive after a ketamine session?

No. Patients cannot drive themselves home after a ketamine session. A designated driver must take you home. Plan a low-demand rest of the day after each session.

For Referring Providers

Delta Healing Solutions handles patient outreach and clinical screening, insurance verification and prior authorization, comprehensive evaluation with Dr. Thomas Rayner, and treatment coordination and progress updates back to the referring provider (with patient consent).

Does Delta Healing Solutions send updates back to the referring provider?

Yes, with patient consent. Referring providers are looped in at treatment start, midpoint, and completion via the contact method requested on the referral form (fax, encrypted email, phone call, or EHR referral loop).

How does Delta Healing Solutions monitor TMS treatment outcomes?

The clinic uses the PHQ-9 as a standardized clinical instrument for baseline symptom burden and to monitor progress. Response is defined as at least a 50% reduction in baseline PHQ-9 score. Remission is defined as a final PHQ-9 score of 4 or lower. After treatment completion, PHQ-9 follow-up surveys are sent every 2-3 months to track durability and to identify symptom recurrence early.
