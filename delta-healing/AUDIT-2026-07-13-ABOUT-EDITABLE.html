About Delta Healing Solutions

Compassionate, combination-treatment mental health care for rural Utah, from a non-profit team that plans care as a whole.

Who We Are

Delta Healing Solutions is a non-profit mental health clinic serving Delta, Fillmore, Oak City, Hinckley, and all of Millard County, Utah. We are committed to making high-quality psychiatric care accessible to rural communities that often lack mental health resources.

Our team combines NeuroStar TMS therapy, ketamine-assisted therapy, medication management, and psychiatric evaluation under one roof, and we coordinate with community talk therapy providers so every patient has access to a full treatment plan, not a single service.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, compassionate, evidence-based mental health care to underserved rural communities in Utah, reduce barriers to treatment, and improve lives through combination-treatment planning and personalized psychiatric care.

How we think about care. TMS works better with talk therapy. Ketamine works better with TMS and talk therapy. Every treatment plan is built around the person, not the service.

Confidential Care in a Small Community

We understand that in a small town, privacy matters. Every appointment is confidential and stays between you and our team. Discretion is part of what we do, and it is one reason people who once avoided care choose to come here.

Non-Profit, Made to Be Affordable

Cost should not decide who can get help. As a non-profit, we accept most major insurance plans (Humana, Select Health, Cigna, Aetna, UMR, United Healthcare, Medicare, PEHP, HMA, Tricare, EMI, and Blue Cross/Blue Shield) and our team handles insurance verification and prior authorization for you.

Medicaid is not currently accepted, but we offer affordable non-profit cash pricing designed specifically for patients without commercial coverage. Ask us. There is almost always a path.

Thomas Rayner, MD

Clinical Director & Psychiatrist

Dr. Rayner is an alumnus of the University of Utah School of Medicine and completed his residency training in adult psychiatry at the University of Nevada, Reno.

A proud member of both the American Psychiatric Association and the Clinical TMS Society, Dr. Rayner is dedicated to the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of a broad spectrum of mental health conditions. He also serves as a medical officer in the Utah Army National Guard.

His expertise encompasses mood disorders, anxiety disorders, psychotic disorders, substance-related disorders, adjustment disorders, and related conditions. Dr. Rayner's clinical focus is on comprehensive, combination-treatment care for individuals and families managing serious mental illness.

Elend S LeBaron

Founder & Executive Director

Elend S LeBaron stands as a distinguished entrepreneur, lauded for his unwavering commitment to excellence and sustainability. His path to success was shaped by the trials of a challenging upbringing within a religious cult, where he witnessed and endured manipulation, mind control, and abuse. Despite these adversities, Elend emerged with a resilient spirit and a deep sense of empathy for others.

His personal journey took a transformative turn when he confronted his own mental health struggles, grappling with severe anxiety, depression, and despair. Despite seeking therapy for years, his battle with depression persisted until he discovered the life-changing benefits of Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS). This breakthrough treatment not only brought color back into his world but also ignited a newfound passion to make it more accessible and affordable for others.

In response to this calling, Elend founded Delta Healing Solutions, a beacon of hope for those seeking relief from mental health challenges. Through the clinic, he aims to provide access to innovative treatments and to create supportive environments where individuals can heal and thrive. His commitment to kindness and sustainability permeates every aspect of his work, reflecting his belief in the power of compassion and community.
