# For Tyla, what I'm working on for Delta Healing

Hey Tyla,

Wanted to give you some insight on what I am doing on my side, aside from spiffing up the actual webpage.

The bigger picture I'm chasing for Delta Healing is this: your growth basically comes from provider referrals, so my job is to make it as easy as possible for a PCP or a psychiatrist to look you up, feel confident, and hit send on that referral. And separately, because it's blowing up faster than most people realize, I want AI tools like ChatGPT and Google's AI answers to recognize Delta Healing as a trusted medical source when patients or doctors ask about TMS in Utah.

Here's what I'm actively working on behind the scenes:

**1. Backing up every clinical claim with real evidence.**
Right now the site says things like "20+ years of research." I'm going through and tying those to specific peer-reviewed studies so a referring doctor can click through and see the actual research. Same treatment for TMS, Ketamine-Assisted Therapy, and Med Management pages. Doesn't change the tone of the site, it just quietly raises the credibility ceiling for anyone who looks closely.

**2. Making Google and AI tools understand who you are.**
This is invisible work. I'm adding structured information behind the scenes that tells search engines and AI tools "this is a legitimate medical organization, with this physician, offering these specific treatments." When ChatGPT or Google's AI answers questions about TMS providers in Utah, this is what puts Delta Healing in the running to be cited.

**3. Making the referral experience smoother for providers.**
Provider inquiries come to info@deltahealingsolutions.org today, which works. What I want to add on top of that: a one-page referral form providers can print or attach to a chart, and (the big one) a system that automatically loops the referring doctor back in when their patient completes treatment. That closing-the-loop step is the single biggest reason a doctor sends a second, third, and tenth patient.

**4. Publishing content that shows up when doctors are researching.**
When a primary care doc asks "should I refer this patient for TMS?", I want the answer to be a Delta Healing article. Building out a small library:
- When to refer a patient for TMS (for primary care)
- How to interpret PHQ-9 scores
- Treatment-resistant depression, when to consider what
- Ketamine-assisted therapy vs. TMS, how to choose
- What actually happens when you refer a patient to Delta Healing

**5. Getting Delta Healing listed everywhere referring doctors and patients look.**
Psychology Today, the NeuroStar provider directory, SAMHSA's national treatment locator, Google Business Profile. Half a day of work per listing, pays back for years.

**6. Building direct relationships with the referring community.**
The best referrals come from doctors who know Dr. Rayner personally. I'll research the top 25 clinicians within 60 miles who could send you patients, help you put together a short educational talk Dr. Rayner can bring to hospital lunches or grand rounds, and set up a quarterly provider newsletter so those relationships stay warm.
